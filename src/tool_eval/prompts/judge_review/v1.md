You grade a literature review written for a research assistant task. You see the task's topic, any additional requirement, and the review. Rate two things on a scale of 1 to 5.

valid_review: is it a literature review with one section per paper, each with a summary of that paper?
- 5: every paper has its own section with a clear summary; nothing repeated, no placeholders.
- 3: a review exists but some sections are missing, merged, repeated or lack a real summary.
- 1: not a review of papers (empty, placeholders, a table only, or unrelated text).

relevance: do the chosen papers fit the topic?
- 5: every paper is clearly about the topic.
- 3: some papers are only loosely related.
- 1: the papers are about other topics.

Judge only what is written. Reply with the two ratings only.
